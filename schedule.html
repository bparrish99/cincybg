Rules
United States Backgammon Federation

Matches use clocks set at 2 minutes for each match point, plus a 12-second delay. Bring a board, a set, and a clock if you have them.

Bracket Match Clock
Winner’s 7 points 14:00 + 12s delay
Consolation 5 points 10:00 + 12s delay
Final 7 points One match, winner takes it
  • The final is one 7-point match between the winner’s-bracket champion and the consolation champion.
  • The consolation winner does not have to win twice. They have already played more matches, and it keeps the day moving.
  • We pay 100% of the entries. Prize amounts depend on the field. First, second, and third place are paid.
  • How late we go depends on the draw — if you are still alive, we could be there into the evening.
