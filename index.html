How we play

The room

What the society is for.

01

Casual vibes

We meet at Marion’s Piazza in Mason. Bring a board and a clock if you have them. Stay as long as your bracket lasts — sometimes into the evening.

02

Competitive spirit

Double elimination, USBGF rules, and clocks on every match. The consolation winner plays one 7-point final — no extra “win twice” hurdle.

03

Unforgettable rolls

The cube gets turned. The board gets noisy. Results land on the recent tournaments page after we pack up the checkers.
